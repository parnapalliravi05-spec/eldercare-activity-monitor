01 • PROJECT OVERVIEW

Automatic activity monitoring

The proposed system uses computer vision and passive IoT monitoring to observe daily activity without requiring the elderly person to manually enter information.

01

Video detection

A camera video can be analysed automatically to detect the presence and movement of a person.

02

Activity counting

Computer vision processing can convert detected movement into measurable activity events and activity duration.

03

Risk awareness

Activity measurements can be compared with an expected pattern to highlight unusual inactivity for caregiver attention.
